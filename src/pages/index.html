The Standard

02 · The Standard We're Measured By

We don't sell countertops. We sell the day your schedule holds.

Every decision in our shop and in the field serves one product: Construction Execution Certainty. A general contractor grades it on four measures, and only four.

Turnover dates kept

Countertops sit on the critical path to unit turns. We build the template, fabrication and install sequence backward from your dates, then hold it.

Punch lists closed

Open punch items block sign-off, retainage and lease-up. Ours get closed while the crew is still in the building, not after the third phone call.

Crews that show

Self-performing crews scheduled to your pull plan, not our convenience. When we commit to a floor, the crew is on it.

Calls returned

One accountable point of contact from preconstruction through closeout. Questions get answers the same day, not the same week.
